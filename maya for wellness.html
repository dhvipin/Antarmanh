    MAYA for wellness

    In today’s high-pressure professional environments, employees are often mentally active yet physically disconnected ,leading to stress, fatigue, and emotional exhaustion. MAYA by Manasthali® bridges this gap by helping teams reconnect with their bodies and restore a sense of calm, focus, and vitality. This body-centered corporate wellness initiative combines movement, mindfulness, and sensory awareness to nurture both mental and physical balance. Grounded in integrative and trauma-informed approaches, MAYA empowers professionals to build emotional resilience, regulate stress, and cultivate a deeper sense of wellbeing in daily work life.

    About the Program

    MAYA is designed to enhance body awareness, emotional regulation, and self-care within the workplace. Through guided exercises and reflective activities, participants learn to release internal tension, regain focus, and reconnect with their natural rhythm.

    Program Highlights:

    • ✔️ Mindful breathwork and grounding movements for stress relief.
    • ✔️ Somatic awareness and emotional mapping for better self-understanding.
    • ✔️ Guided reflection, journaling, and creative body-based expression.
    • ✔️ Therapeutic use of sound, scent, light, and visuals for relaxation.
    • ✔️ Each practice supports nervous system regulation and emotional balance, translating into improved engagement, empathy, and performance at work.

    Corporate Applications

    • ✔️ MAYA can be customized to suit your organization’s structure and goals:
    • ✔️ Team Wellbeing Workshops – Interactive sessions promoting collective calm, creativity, and connection.
    • ✔️ Leadership Programs – For enhancing emotional intelligence, presence, and grounded decision-making.
    • ✔️ Wellness Retreats or Off-Site Sessions – Deeply restorative experiences for relaxation and renewal.
    • ✔️ Workplace Reset Modules – Short, sensory-based breaks during work hours to enhance focus and recharge.

    Benefits for Organizations

    • ✔️ Reduces employee stress and burnout.
    • ✔️ Improves emotional intelligence and communication.
    • ✔️ Enhances creativity, focus, and problem-solving.
    • ✔️ Builds resilience and adaptability to change.
    • ✔️ Promotes a culture of mindfulness and holistic wellbeing.
