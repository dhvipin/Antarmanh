    Internship Program

    Hands-on learning for UG and PG Psychology students at Manasthali

    Welcome to Our Internship Program

    At Manasthali, we are committed to nurturing the next generation of mental health professionals. Our Internship Program is carefully designed for Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) psychology students who want to gain practical exposure, clinical insights, and hands-on learning beyond the classroom.

    We believe that real learning happens when theory meets practice—and our internship creates exactly that bridge.

    Program Objectives

    • Understand how therapy and psychiatric consultations are conducted in real-world clinical settings.
    • Learn to apply classroom knowledge in a professional environment.
    • Gain exposure to a variety of mental health concerns and therapeutic approaches.
    • Build foundational skills in case history taking, MSE (Mental Status Examination), psychological assessments, and clinical observation.
    • Develop confidence in interacting with professionals and eventually with clients.

    What You Will Learn & Experience

    1. Clinical Observation
    • Observe licensed psychologists, therapists, and psychiatrists in action.
    • Get a first-hand look at OPD sessions, therapy sessions, and multidisciplinary teamwork.
    • Understand how professionals build rapport, ask the right questions, and guide clients.
    2. Case-Based Learning
    • Learn about different mental health disorders through live cases and discussions.
    • Participate in case study presentations and discussions led by supervisors.
    • Understand how diagnosis is made, and how treatment plans are created.
    3. Skill Training
    • Training in case history taking and MSE (Mental Status Examination).
    • Exposure to psychological assessments (cognitive, personality, clinical tools).
    • Learn documentation, observation, and reflective writing.
    4. Interactive Learning
    • Engage in Q&A sessions with our experts.
    • Small group interactions for peer learning and discussions.
    5. Professional Exposure
    • Learn about the ethics of clinical practice.
    • Understand how to work in a team-based setting (psychologists, psychiatrists, counselors).
    • Explore different therapeutic modalities (CBT, family therapy, integrative approaches, etc.).

    Program Details

    • Duration: 1 month (batches begin every 15 days)
    • Timing: Flexible, designed to fit student schedules
    • Mode: In-person at our clinic, with hybrid options if available
    • Eligibility: UG & PG psychology students (all specializations)
    • Fees: ₹7,000 per month
