Psychiatry

Comprehensive Psychiatric Care

At Manasthali, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care that combines modern medical treatment with emotional support and psychoeducation, helping individuals manage mental health concerns with dignity, compassion, and clinical expertise.

Psychiatry OPD Services

Our Outpatient Psychiatry Department (OPD) provides evaluation, diagnosis, and evidence-based treatment for a wide range of mental health conditions.

We follow an integrative approach that combines medication management, psychotherapy, and lifestyle guidance.

Psychiatric Diagnosis and Management

We provide assessment and treatment for:

  • Major Depressive Disorder
  • Bipolar Affective Disorder
  • Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD)
  • Generalized Anxiety Disorder & Panic Disorder
  • Schizophrenia and other Psychotic Disorders
  • Substance Use and Addiction Disorders
  • Others (ADHD, Psychosexual disorders, etc.)

Each treatment plan is personalized and may involve medication, therapy, and supportive care.

Psychoeducation

We believe awareness is the first step to healing. Our team offers structured psychoeducation sessions for individuals and families to:

  • Understand the nature of the illness
  • Learn about treatment options
  • Develop coping strategies
  • Reduce stigma and improve compliance

These sessions help you and your loved ones become active participants in the recovery journey.

Psychotherapy

Our psychiatrists work closely with clinical psychologists and psychotherapists to provide:

  • Individual Therapy
  • Family & Couples Therapy
  • Crisis Intervention
  • Supportive Therapy
  • CBT and other evidence-based approaches

Therapy is offered alongside or independent of medication, based on the client’s needs.

Special Clinics

We offer focused care through our specialized psychiatric clinics, designed to provide in-depth assessment, treatment, and follow-up:

OCD

Targeted interventions for Obsessive Compulsive Disorder with a combination of medication and Exposure-Response Prevention (ERP) therapy.

Psychosexual Disorders

Assessment and management of sexual dysfunctions, performance anxiety, and relationship-related issues with discretion and sensitivity.

Personality Disorders

Comprehensive diagnosis, emotional regulation strategies, and long-term psychotherapy support for individuals with personality-related concerns.

Behavioral Addictions

Treatment for non-substance-based addictions like internet, gaming, pornography, or gambling through behavior therapy and relapse prevention strategies.
