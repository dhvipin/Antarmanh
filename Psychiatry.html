🧠 Psychiatry Services

Holistic, Compassionate & Evidence-based Mental Health Care

Comprehensive Psychiatric Care

At Manasthali, we offer comprehensive psychiatric care that combines modern medical treatment with emotional support and psychoeducation, helping individuals manage mental health concerns with dignity and clinical expertise.

Psychiatry OPD Services

Our Outpatient Psychiatry Department (OPD) provides evaluation, diagnosis, and evidence-based treatment for a wide range of mental health conditions through an integrative approach of medication, therapy, and lifestyle guidance.

Psychiatric Diagnosis and Management

We provide assessment and treatment for:

  • Major Depressive Disorder
  • Bipolar Affective Disorder
  • Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD)
  • Generalized Anxiety Disorder & Panic Disorder
  • Schizophrenia and Psychotic Disorders
  • Substance Use & Addiction Disorders
  • Others (ADHD, Psychosexual disorders, etc.)

Psychoeducation

We believe awareness is the first step to healing. Our psychoeducation sessions help families:

  • Understand the illness
  • Learn treatment options
  • Develop coping strategies
  • Reduce stigma and improve compliance

Psychotherapy

  • Individual Therapy
  • Family & Couples Therapy
  • Crisis Intervention
  • Supportive Therapy
  • CBT and evidence-based approaches

Special Clinics

We offer focused care through specialized clinics for:

OCD: ERP-based treatment for obsessive thoughts & rituals.
Psychosexual Disorders: Sensitive assessment & therapy for intimacy and performance issues.
Personality Disorders: Emotional regulation and long-term psychotherapy.
Behavioral Addictions: Therapy for gaming, internet, or gambling addictions.
