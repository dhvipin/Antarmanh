    MAYA

    A Gentle Journey to Reconnect with Your Body

    Explore More

    About the Program

    MAYA is a body-centered wellness experience that helps individuals build a deeper connection with their physical and emotional selves. Grounded in integrative, trauma-informed approaches, this module invites participants to explore their body’s inner landscape through movement, breath, reflection, and sensory support.

    This phase focuses on nurturing body awareness, emotional expression, and intuitive self-care, offering a safe space for grounding, release, and healing.

    What You’ll Experience

    Mindful Breathwork

    Use of breath and gentle movement to reduce stress and center the mind.

    Somatic Mapping

    Build awareness of body sensations and emotions through guided journaling.

    Creative Expression

    Explore movement, visuals, and sound for emotional clarity and resilience.

    The MAYA Experience

    • 🎵 Sound: Tanpura music, singing bowls
    • 🌺 Scent: Lavender, sandalwood, jasmine
    • 🌄 Visuals: Nature imagery, mandalas
    • 💡 Lighting: Soft yellow or natural light
    • 🎨 Colors: Earthy tones and calming palettes

    Who Is This For?

    This experience is for anyone who wants to reconnect with their body and emotions.

    • ✔️ Reconnect with their body
    • ✔️ Cultivate emotional calm
    • ✔️ Develop self-awareness
    • ✔️ Release tension and stress
    • ✔️ Explore healing in a nurturing space
