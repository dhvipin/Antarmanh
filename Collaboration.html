DR. RICHA DHAMIJA

DENTIST | CLINICAL HYPNOTHERAPIST

She is a practicing dental surgeon with 27 years of experience at various prestigious hospitals. I am running my multi-speciality dental clinic HOUSE OF SMILES along with a team of highly qualified and experienced Dental specialists in Delhi. I have a keen focus on introducing teaching and training DENTAL HYPNOSIS to Dentists in India as a very niche subject. Hypnosis is a powerful non-pharmaceutical tool that is used in dentistry. The use of hypnosis in dentistry is not widely used throughout the dental profession, especially in our country. She established House of Smiles in 2010 along with a highly qualified team of fine specialists including Orthodontist, Prosthodontist, Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon and Implant specialist. We strive to provide multidisciplinary and holistic management of various dental problems to achieve full mouth oral rehabilitation. Using quality dental materials, highly trained specialists, each with at least 25 years of experience, they execute quality care in a very compassionate environment for the patients. She acquired her Diploma in Dental and Medical hypnosis from the UK with a passion to bring and introduce the benefits of Hypnotherapy to her patients. HopeNest was established with the same dream of making awareness of Hypnotherapy in dentistry and well as in other fields.

Ms. Mehak Thukral

MA (Clinical Psychology) | Assistant Psychologist

Ms. Mehak Thukral is a creative, non judgemental and a proactive individual. She has attended numerous workshops on varied themes of psychology and has received experience through various internships. She is keen on learning, and is empathetic. She keeps interest in expressive arts therapy and psycho-oncology and further want to amalgamate expressive arts in her practice with Neuro-divergent population and as well as in the community mental well-being. She has been a part of different mental health volunteering programs, working at the grassroot level and is inclined to work towards spreading awareness and sensitizing people in the field of mental health. She is also managing Manasthali’s Mental Health Support helpline and guiding people with stress, anxiety and mental health concerns.

Ms. Poonam Khurana

Yoga Acharya | Master of Yogic Science

Poonam Khurana is a dedicated Yoga Acharya and the founder of Poonam Yogasana, a well-regarded yoga school rooted in the traditional wisdom of Rishikesh and Gurugram. With over 15 years of experience, she integrates classical yogic principles with a modern, holistic understanding of health and well-being. Her teaching philosophy emphasizes mindful movement, correct alignment, and self-awareness ,guiding individuals to connect deeply with both body and mind. At Poonam Yogasana, she fosters an inclusive, nurturing environment where each student is supported in their personal growth and healing journey. Poonam’s approach reflects her belief that yoga is not just a practice but a way of life — a path towards balance, clarity, and inner harmony.
